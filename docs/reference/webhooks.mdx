title: Webhooks and events
description: Signed calls to your server when something happens on a partner account
---

**Partner accounts only.** Widgetic does not send webhooks to ordinary user or organization keys. Self-serve developers use the REST API and dashboard; partners can receive the same facts by email, in the dashboard inbox, and as signed HTTP calls to their own server.

## What you receive

Each event is a `POST` with JSON body `{ id, type, createdAt, partnerId, data }` and Standard Webhooks headers (`webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature`). Event types include invoices, credit warnings, partner standing changes, and (webhook-only) activity on your managed users: compositions, widgets, uploads, entries and workflows.

The full event table, payload shapes, retry schedule and verification steps are in [Partner notifications](/docs/partners/notifications).

## Configure

- **Dashboard:** notification settings for your partner account.
- **API:** `GET/PUT /partners/{partnerId}/notifications/settings`, `POST …/webhook-secret`, `POST …/test`, delivery log and replay — see [Partners API](/docs/cli/commands/api-partners) and the OpenAPI tag `Partners`.

## Verify and operate safely

Read [Webhook signatures](/resources/security#webhook-signatures) and [Verify it](/docs/partners/notifications#verify-it) before you act on traffic in production:

- Verify the signature on the **raw** body.
- Reject timestamps older than five minutes.
- Remember `webhook-id` to drop duplicates (delivery is at least once).
- Ignore unknown `type` values with `2xx` so new events do not break your receiver.
